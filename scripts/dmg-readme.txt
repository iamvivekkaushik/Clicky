Clicky — control a Samsung TV from the Mac menu bar

Needs macOS 13 or later and a Samsung Smart TV (Tizen, 2016 or newer) on the
same Wi-Fi. Made for The Frame; Art Mode features only appear on a Frame.

INSTALL
1. Drag Clicky onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Clicky from Applications. It lives in the menu bar (a small TV icon),
   not the Dock.

FIRST OPEN
Clicky isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer, so macOS
stops it the first time.

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later:
    Open it once and choose Done. Then open System Settings > Privacy &
    Security, scroll down to "Clicky was blocked", click Open Anyway and
    confirm.

  macOS 13 (Ventura) and 14 (Sonoma):
    In Applications, Control-click Clicky, choose Open, then Open again.

You only do this once. If macOS asks whether Clicky may find devices on your
local network, choose Allow; it can't see the TV otherwise.

SET UP THE TV
- Turn the TV on. Clicky lists the Samsung TVs it finds; pick yours.
- The TV asks whether to allow Clicky. Choose Allow with the TV remote.
- To turn the TV on from the Mac, enable this on the TV:
  Settings > General > Network > Expert Settings > Power On with Mobile.
- Several TVs: click the small arrows next to the TV name to add or switch.

Clicky talks to the TV only on your local network. No account, no cloud.
